## Cross-Border E-commerce Payment Cost and Settlement Worksheet

### A practical worksheet for identifying payment-conversion leakage, hidden payment costs, settlement exposure and reconciliation burden across African markets.

### What this worksheet helps you decide

This is not only a fee calculator. It helps you determine whether your current payment setup is affecting completed sales, net settled value, cash availability or the amount of finance and operations work required to manage payments.

Use actual transaction and settlement data wherever possible. If a figure is estimated, mark it as an estimate. Compare the same period across providers, countries, payment methods and settlement destinations.

## How to use the worksheet

- Choose a consistent review period, ideally at least one complete month
- List every customer market included in the review
- Separate successful, failed, refunded and disputed transactions
- Use provider settlement reports, order data and accounting records
- Calculate net settled value, not only the advertised processing fee
- Review the results by market and payment method
- Identify the largest commercial problem before changing providers

### Calculations

Payment completion rate = successful payments ÷ payment attempts. Failure rate = failed or declined attempts ÷ payment attempts. Retry recovery rate = payments recovered after retry ÷ failed or declined attempts. Attempted order value at risk = failed attempts × average order value.

### What this tells us

- Which market has the weakest payment completion?
- Which payment method causes the most failures?
- Are failed payments being recovered through retries or alternatives?
- Are customers left in pending status?
- Is a cheaper route producing less completed order value?

## 9. Diagnosis: what is the main problem?

Use your results to identify the primary issue. Select one main diagnosis and record the evidence behind it.

- ☐ Payment-conversion problem: customers are failing or abandoning payment
- ☐ Payment-method coverage problem: important customer methods are missed
- ☐ Margin problem: fees, FX or deductions reduce net settled value
- ☐ Settlement problem: funds arrive too slowly or unpredictably
- ☐ Reconciliation problem: finance spends too much time matching records
- ☐ Provider-fragmentation problem: several providers create unnecessary issues
- ☐ Expansion-readiness problem: the current setup cannot support the next market

## What a useful result looks like

By completing this worksheet, you should be able to explain where payment performance is affecting completed sales, where payment and settlement costs are reducing retained value, how much operational work the setup creates and which issue should be addressed first
