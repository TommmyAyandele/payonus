**Scoring key**

- 0 — Not confirmed: The provider did not answer clearly or cannot meet the requirement
- 1 — Partially confirmed: The capability exists but has limitations, conditions, or unresolved questions
- 2 — Confirmed: The provider gave a clear answer and supporting evidence

**Decision summary**

**Strong candidate if:**

- ☐ Forex eligibility is confirmed
- ☐ All priority markets are supported
- ☐ Deposits and payouts are both covered
- ☐ Settlement works with our treasury requirements
- ☐ Compliance responsibilities are clear
- ☐ Integration requirements are manageable
- ☐ Reporting and reconciliation are sufficient
- ☐ Commercial terms are understood
- ☐ Support and incident handling are credible

**Do not proceed until:**

- ☐ Eligibility is confirmed in writing
- ☐ Payout capability is confirmed separately from deposits
- ☐ Settlement and reserve terms are documented
- ☐ Compliance responsibilities are understood
- ☐ Technical testing is complete
- ☐ Reconciliation has been validated
- ☐ Total cost is understood

**Red flags**

Be cautious if:

- ☐ The provider says only we support Africa without naming markets
- ☐ Forex eligibility is unclear or only verbally confirmed
- ☐ Deposits are explained but withdrawals are vague
- ☐ Settlement timing or currency is not disclosed
- ☐ Reserve or fund-hold terms are unclear
- ☐ No sandbox or API documentation is available
- ☐ Transaction statuses are not documented
- ☐ Reconciliation reports are unavailable
- ☐ The provider cannot explain who handles compliance
- ☐ There is no incident-escalation process
- ☐ Fees are presented only as one headline rate
- ☐ The provider cannot explain what happens if its risk policy changes

**Questions for your next provider conversation**

1. Can you confirm our Forex eligibility for each target market?
2. Which deposit methods are available in each market?
3. Which payout methods are available in each market?
4. What are the settlement currencies and timelines?
5. What reserves, limits, and fund-hold conditions apply?
6. What happens when a deposit or payout fails?
7. How will transactions reconcile with our trading platform?
8. What compliance documents will you require?
9. What does implementation and testing involve?
10. Who supports us after go-live?
