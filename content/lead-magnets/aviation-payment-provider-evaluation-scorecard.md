# AVIATION PAYMENT PROVIDER EVALUATION SCORECARD

### Compare Payment Providers With Evidence

*A practical decision tool for airlines, OTAs, travel agencies, flight aggregators and other travel businesses operating across African markets.*

## What This Scorecard Helps You Decide

Choosing a payment provider based primarily on transaction fees can hide the operational factors that affect bookings, revenue and finance.

This scorecard helps you compare providers across:

- Payment coverage
- Payment performance and confirmation
- Refunds, disputes and chargebacks
- Fraud and risk management
- Settlement
- Reconciliation and reporting
- API and developer experience
- Support and operations
- Scalability and business fit
- Commercial and contractual fit

The objective is to identify the provider that best fits your actual payment operation while exposing any gaps that need to be resolved before you commit.

**Important:** A provider that scores well overall should not automatically be selected if it fails a requirement you have marked Must Have.

## HOW TO USE THIS SCORECARD

Work through the scorecard in this order:

**1. Document your payment operation**

Understand your markets, payment methods, transaction profile and current problems.

**2. Define your requirements**

Separate what is essential from what is simply desirable.

**3. Evaluate each provider against the same requirements**

Do not change your evaluation criteria from one provider to another.

**4. Record the evidence**

Use documentation, sandbox testing, written confirmation, contract terms or reliable merchant references wherever possible.

**5. Score each requirement**

Use the same 1–5 scoring standard for every provider.

**6. Check for critical gaps**

A high score does not compensate for a failed Must Have requirement.

**7. Compare the providers**

Look at strengths, weaknesses, unresolved questions and critical gaps together.

**8. Make the decision**

Record why the selected provider fits and what must be resolved before implementation.

| Scalability & Business Fit | Ability to support additional markets, payment methods and transaction volume as the business grows |

## 5. SCORING SYSTEM

Use the same scoring standard for every provider.

| Score | What it means |
|---|---|
| 1 — Major Gap | The provider does not meet the requirement, or there is insufficient evidence to rely on the capability |
| 2 — Weak Fit | Some capability exists, but significant limitations remain |
| 3 — Acceptable | The requirement appears to be supported, but important details still need clarification |
| 4 — Strong | The provider clearly supports the requirement with relatively few concerns |
| 5 — Verified Fit | The capability has been demonstrated through documentation, testing, contractual confirmation or another reliable source |

## A FINAL CHECK BEFORE YOU COMMIT

Ask yourself:

Can this provider support the markets and payment methods our passengers actually need?

Can our booking system reliably know what happened to each payment?

Can finance trace the transaction from booking through payment and settlement?

Can the provider handle the situations that happen when payments do not go as expected?

Have we verified the capabilities that matter most to our business?

Are any Must Have requirements still unresolved?

If the answer to the final question is **yes**, the evaluation is not finished.
